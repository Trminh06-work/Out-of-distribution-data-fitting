\begin{frame}{Single Hyperball}
    A Hyperball, or ball $\mathbf B_r(\mathbf w)$ of radius $r$ about $\mathbf w \in \mathbb R^n$ is defined as:
    \[
    \mathbf B_r(\mathbf w) \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \left\{\mathbf x \in \mathbb R^n \mid d(\mathbf x, \mathbf w) \le r\right\}
    \]
